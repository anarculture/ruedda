The problem

800,000 motorcycles a year. Formal credit for them is practically nonexistent.

A motorcycle isn't a lifestyle choice in Venezuela — it's the tool people use to earn a living. Demand is proven; financing is the missing piece.

A collapsed banking system

Legal reserve requirements far above regional peers (Brazil 25%, Colombia 11%, Peru 3%) keep Venezuelan banks structurally unable to lend at scale.

No usable credit history

Years of hyperinflation and currency turnover erased the data trail banks would normally underwrite against.

Regulated rates that ignore real risk

Where bank credit exists, capped interest rates do not reflect actual default risk, so banks avoid the segment instead of pricing for it.

The solution

A leasing company, not a lender

RUEDDA structures every contract as an operating lease with an initial payment, retaining title to the motorcycle for the full contract term. This single structural choice changes everything downstream.

Outside banking supervision

As a leasing structure rather than a loan, RUEDDA is not subject to the interest-rate caps and lending regulation that keep banks out of this market.

Priced for real risk

Because we are not rate-capped, RUEDDA can charge a margin that reflects the credit risk of an unbanked, informal-economy customer base.

RUEDDA owns the asset

Title stays with RUEDDA until the lease ends. That single fact is what makes GPS tracking, kill-switch recovery, and full insurance coverage possible.

Competitive landscape

Everyone touches this market. No one owns it.

Banks

Not participating

Rate caps make motorcycle lending unattractive versus cars, which banks are only beginning to finance.

BNPL: Cashea, Krece

Adjacent, not competing

Four-installment, roughly six-week terms for small tickets, down payments only. No title, no GPS, no recovery mechanism.

Brand-owned financing

Single-brand only

Bera Services, Toro × Rapicredit, Bel Cuotas. Locked to one manufacturer's catalog, no cross-brand credit engine.

Dealer-leasers

Inventory-constrained

Glik, Saba Transporte. Leasing tied to on-hand stock, no dedicated tech or scoring layer.

Other leasing companies: Chekia, MotosYes

Closest comparable model

Approval takes 48 to 96 hours through manual review, and initial payments run higher than RUEDDA's.

RUEDDA's position: brand-agnostic across ESCUDA, Empire Keeway, and future partners, plus instant tech-driven approval and hardware-based risk management.
